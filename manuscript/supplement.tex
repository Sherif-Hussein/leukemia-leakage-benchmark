\caption{Model overview. Each cell is described by engineered features, a frozen CNN embedding (optionally reduced by PCA), their fusion, or a partially fine-tuned CNN. Cell-level classifiers serve T1, T2 and T4. For patients and subjects (T3, T5), cells are combined by pooled statistics, by gated attention MIL or by averaging fine-tuned cell probabilities, and the three are combined in the pre-specified ensemble. The decision threshold is set on development data for a recall of at least 0.95.}
\label{fig:model}
\end{figure}
